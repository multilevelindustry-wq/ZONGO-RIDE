How drivers can support respectful, non-emergency journeys

Drivers who accept a healthcare-related trip should confirm the agreed pickup and destination, arrive at the agreed time where possible, drive responsibly and communicate delays promptly. They should treat the passenger with dignity, protect privacy and avoid asking intrusive questions. A driver should never claim to diagnose a condition, provide clinical care, lift or transfer a passenger without appropriate ability and agreement, or promise that a journey is medically safe. If the passenger needs support beyond an ordinary ride, the driver should be honest about the limits of the service.

For passengers, the most important point is to confirm the service before travelling. If a vehicle, assistance level or timing requirement is essential, do not rely on an assumption. Ask a clear question and proceed only when the arrangement is suitable.

Frequently asked questions about chronic kidney disease and dialysis and Zongo Ride

Can Zongo Ride provide medical treatment?

No. Zongo Ride is a ride marketplace and does not diagnose, treat, monitor or provide medical advice. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions.

Can I use an ordinary ride for every medical appointment?

Not necessarily. The passenger should follow the care team’s advice. Some people may need a specialised accessible vehicle, supervised transport or medical transport that a standard ride cannot provide.

Can a driver help with a wheelchair, walker or medical equipment?

Do not assume so. Ask about the exact vehicle and assistance before booking. Confirm that any mobility equipment can be transported safely and that the driver can provide only the assistance they are actually able and willing to provide.

What if symptoms become urgent before pickup?

Do not wait for the ride. Contact local emergency services or follow the emergency instructions given by the healthcare team.

Should I tell the driver my diagnosis?

Usually, share only the practical information needed for the trip. You can explain a pickup, access or seating requirement without disclosing private medical details.
