ZONGO RIDE BLOG PACKAGE

- images/: 20 original lightweight SVG illustrations
- style.css: responsive styling
- sitemap.xml: sitemap with homepage and all article pages

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. In every file, replace https://zongo.shop with the actual public Zongo Ride website domain if it differs.
2. Upload the files to your website host. Keep index.html at the blog root, articles/ and images/ beside it.
3. The article URLs end in .HTML as requested. Hosting must preserve uppercase extensions; links are generated consistently.
4. Search engines cannot be guaranteed to index pages. Verify your domain in Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml.
5. The articles are practical starter content, not a guarantee of rankings or traffic. Review and expand with real service coverage, locations, policies, and original experience before publication.
6. All illustrations are original local SVG files and do not need external image hosting.
