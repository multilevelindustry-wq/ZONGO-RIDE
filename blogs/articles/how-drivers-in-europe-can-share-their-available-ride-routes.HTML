Why clear online information matters

People searching for a ride usually want to know whether a driver covers their route, where pickup can happen, when the journey is possible and how much it may cost. A useful driver listing answers these questions honestly. It also reduces avoidable misunderstandings before a booking request is made.

Common questions from drivers

Can I register if I am an independent driver?

Review the current Zongo Ride registration requirements. If independent drivers are accepted in your location, provide the requested personal, licence and vehicle information and complete each verification step.

Will joining guarantee that I receive passengers?

No. Registration and route listings do not guarantee requests, income or a particular number of passengers. Demand varies by location, timing, route and passenger preferences.

Can I list more than one route?

If your account dashboard supports multiple routes, add each route separately with accurate pickup areas, destinations, prices and availability. Keep every listing current.

What should I do before accepting a request?

Confirm the pickup point, destination, departure time, passenger requirements and agreed price. Check that the trip is legal, safe and practical for your vehicle and schedule.
